## Idea

- [ ] The steep early drop is the real argument for annotating *while* reading rather than after: the cost of waiting is paid in the first hour. — “The curve he drew drops steeply in the first hours, then flattens” [↩](obsidian://hypomnema?vault=demo-vault&file=Why%20we%20forget%20what%20we%20read.md&id=a1)
- [ ] This is the core idea: annotation as dialogue, not storage.
  Links with Montaigne's method — reading as a conversation that produces one's own text. — “They were the trace of a dialogue between the reader and the text” [↩](obsidian://hypomnema?vault=demo-vault&file=Why%20we%20forget%20what%20we%20read.md&id=a3)
  ↳ 2026-10-30 Second reading: the same applies to legal or scientific texts, not only literature.
