The margin is part of level 1: nothing to configure beyond turning it on. It is off by default and changes nothing in how comments are stored.

## Turning it on

Turn on *Comments in the margin* in *Settings → Hypomnema*, or run the command *Show or hide comments in the margin*. Each comment then sits beside its passage, in the colour of its category.

Hovering a margin note highlights its passage. Clicking it opens the thread in the panel, where you still write and reply: the margin is for reading, the panel for writing.

The *Margin content* setting shows either the comment alone or the whole thread.

![Turning on the margin from the command palette, then opening a thread](/screenshots/07-margin-en.gif)

## Narrow panes

When the pane is too narrow for a margin (side panel open, small screen, *Readable line length* turned off), each comment moves below its paragraph instead.

![On a narrow pane, comments sit below their paragraph](/screenshots/08-margin-narrow-en.png)

## Limits

Margin comments appear in Live Preview and source mode, not in reading view.

The method note [The margin](/method/margin/) explains the reading practice behind this feature.
