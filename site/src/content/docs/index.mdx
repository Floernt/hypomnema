## A comment is plain text in the note

Hypomnema is an Obsidian plugin for reading, reflection and knowledge organisation. You comment on a passage, or on a precise point, of any note. You write, reply and navigate in a side panel, much like the comments pane in Word. Comments go into coloured categories linked to tags. They are stored as plain text inside the note: searchable, synced with the rest of your vault, readable without the plugin.

The name comes from the Greek *hypomnēmata*, the notebooks in which readers copied passages and wrote down their own remarks so they could come back to them.

## Light by default, deep on demand

The plugin is organised in three levels. Each one appears only if you go looking for it. Without any action defined, the plugin behaves as it always has.

<Steps>

2. **Comment** on a passage of any note: [your first comment](/getting-started/first-comment/).
3. **Extract** your comments when the reading is over: [extraction](/concepts/extraction/).
